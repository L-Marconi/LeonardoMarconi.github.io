My research is concerned with stochastic dynamics and statistical inference on spaces with non-Euclidean geometry, including stratified and singular spaces.

## Stochastic processes on stratified spaces

I study Markov processes that combine diffusion within smooth strata with transitions between strata, as well as Brownian motion on singular spaces and limits of stochastic systems near interfaces.

## Statistical inference and latent structures

A central theme of my current work is how the geometry of a parameter space affects inference. This includes hierarchical copula models, latent tree structures, and extremal dependence, with an emphasis on statistical resolution, topology recovery, and uncertainty quantification near singularities.

## Geometric statistics

I am interested in the geometry of covariance matrices of fixed or varying rank, including Bures–Wasserstein geometry and its implications for stochastic modelling.

## Stochastic partial differential equations

My work also includes stochastic hyperbolic equations and their interaction with geometric methods.
