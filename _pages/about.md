I am **Leonardo Marconi**. I hold a **PhD in Statistics from the University of Bologna (2026)**, and my research lies at the intersection of **probability, mathematical statistics, and geometry**.

My research explores how the geometry of a space influences stochastic processes and statistical inference, particularly when the underlying state space or parameter space is singular or stratified.

My main research interests include:

- **Stochastic processes on stratified spaces:** diffusions and jump–diffusions with transitions between strata, and scaling limits near interfaces.
- **Statistical inference and latent structures:** geometric approaches to hierarchical models, copulas, and latent trees, including inference when the topology is not fully resolved.
- **Geometric statistics:** geometry of low-rank covariance matrices and Bures–Wasserstein structures.
- **Stochastic partial differential equations:** in particular, stochastic hyperbolic equations.

You can read more about these topics on the [Research](/research/) page. My [Publications](/publications/), [Talks](/talks/), and [CV](/cv/) provide further information about my academic work.
